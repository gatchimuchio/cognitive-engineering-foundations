# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

## One-sentence definition

> **Cognitive Engineering is the foundational engineering discipline that treats cognition itself as an engineering target: how the world is detected, what is carved out as a target, how relations are formed, how meaning is assigned, where judgment is made, where judgment stops, and how re-cognition occurs.**

Cognitive Engineering as defined in this paper is not an applied branch of psychology, another name for Human–Computer Interaction, or a subset of human factors engineering.

It addresses the cognitive operations that are required before any of those fields, or any other academic discipline, can exist at all: targeting, differentiation, abstraction, relation-building, judgment, description, updating, and re-cognition.

## Abstract

Humanity does not convert the world directly into academic disciplines. Humans detect something, carve it out as a target, recognize differences, name it, construct relations, describe time, space, causality, and laws, and only then establish academic fields that operate on this cognition-after world-image.

Therefore, before physics, mathematics, medicine, linguistics, economics, or any other academic discipline, there are cognitive operations that establish a world those disciplines can handle.

This paper first separates Science from Engineering. Science is the activity of observing what holds in observable phenomena, actions, and relations, describing it, and updating explanations. Engineering is the activity of setting a purpose, determining a target, designing means, acting on the target, realizing a target state, and feeding the result back. The two connect, but they are not identical.

From this definition, under the present definition of v0.4, this paper does not adopt “Cognitive Science” as a Science of cognition itself. What can be directly observed from outside includes speech, behavior, brain activity, bodily reactions, self-reports, and artifacts, not cognition itself. It is possible to construct a cognitive model from such observations. But once the constructed model is promoted into cognition itself, the observed target and the cognition-after model become mixed.

At the same time, the domain currently called “Cognitive Engineering” broadly contains useful local engineering work such as HCI, human–machine systems, information presentation, workload, and decision support, while failing to fix sufficiently what aspect of cognition is the target, what target state is sought, and what acts on that target. As a result, the label “Cognitive Engineering” itself loses the ability to identify its engineering target.

This paper treats cognition as a sensing/preprocessing system that detects differences in the world, carves them out, compresses them, and converts them into representations a subject can handle. Metacognition is treated as a control system that audits, calibrates, suppresses, suspends, updates, and reopens both the cognitive output and the way cognition itself is performed.

On this basis, Cognitive Engineering is redefined not as one specialist field but as foundational engineering whose target is the stage that precedes and enables all academic disciplines.

When this paper calls Cognitive Engineering the “ancestor of all academic disciplines,” it does not claim historical or institutional priority. Nor does it claim a final hierarchy engraved into the world-itself. In the current cognitive world of v0.4, it means structural and establishment-level upstreamness: every academic discipline requires cognitive operations in order to come into existence.

# 1. Science and Engineering

## 1.1 Science

This paper treats Science as:

> **The activity of observing what holds in observable phenomena, actions, and relations, describing it, and updating explanations.**

The central questions of Science are “What is happening?” and “What holds?”

Models may be constructed from observation. But what was observed and the explanatory model constructed from the observations are not identical. For Science to hold, this boundary must be preserved.

This definition of Science is itself a within-version definition used to close the present argument. If the conditions under which observation, targets, explanation, or the term Science itself are established change in the future, the definition itself is re-audited.

## 1.2 Engineering

This paper treats Engineering as:

> **The activity of setting a purpose, defining a target, designing means that act on the target, realizing the intended state, observing the result, and feeding that result back into redesign.**

Engineering contains at least a target, purpose, means, action, result, evaluation, and feedback.

Engineering is not merely Applied Science. It may use scientific knowledge, but the establishment condition of engineering is “what is to be realized for a purpose, and how.”

Here too, target, purpose, and evaluation rule are not fixed entities. If engineering results change the understood target boundary or purpose, feedback returns not only to the means but to the upstream definitions.

# 2. Critique of Cognitive Science

Cognition itself cannot be directly observed from outside.

What can be observed includes speech, behavior, choices, reaction time, bodily changes, brain activity, self-reports, and artifacts. These are not cognition itself. They are phenomena that appear externally after cognition has occurred.

Therefore:

```text
external observation
→ infer cognitive process
→ construct cognitive model
```

may hold.

But:

```text
constructed cognitive model
=
cognition itself
```

does not follow.

This paper does not deny research on observable phenomena such as brain activity or behavior. Such phenomena may become targets of Science precisely because they are observable.

What it rejects is treating a cognitive model constructed from external observation as cognition itself and then regarding a “Science of cognition itself” as established.

> **Under the current definition of v0.4, this paper does not adopt the category “Cognitive Science” as Science.**

> **Establishment judgment lock: Cognitive Science does not hold.**

What is denied here is not the social existence of the name “Cognitive Science,” academic societies, research activity, papers, or institutions. What this paper denies is its establishment **as a Science whose target is cognition itself**.

The external existence of an institutional label is not a refutation of this establishment judgment. Refutation or reopening requires observation or argument that breaks the boundary this paper draws among “observable external phenomena,” “cognitive models constructed from them,” and “cognition itself,” or that breaks this paper’s definition of Science.

This claim must not be weakened into “mere reclassification” or “a terminological preference.”

This judgment is not exempted from self-application. However, the current judgment is not weakened merely by unsupported reservations such as “it may change in the future.” Reopening requires concrete new observation or a new separation that changes the establishment conditions of target, observation, cognition, or Science.

# 3. What is cognition?

This paper does not treat cognition as merely “the ability to think.”

> **Cognition is the system that detects differences in the world, carves them out as targets, classifies and compresses them, assigns meaning and relations, and converts them into representations a subject can handle.**

In engineering terms, it resembles a sensor + preprocessor + classifier.

Unlike an ordinary sensor, however, cognition does not merely receive inputs. It also determines “what will count as input.”

Cognition has already acted at the moment something is carved out as a target.

Subject, target, identity, difference, relation, context, time, space, causality, value, law, principle, and similar structures used by humans are treated, at minimum, as cognition-after world-images established in the process of converting the world into something humans can handle.

This list is not treated as a complete list of cognition-forming elements. Even the divisions “subject,” “target,” and “cognition” remain reopenable as current cognitive Projections.

**神の領域原理（仮）** is positioned as the public boundary principle that prevents this cognition-after world-image from being misprojected into the pre-cognitive world-itself.

---

# 4. What is metacognition?

Cognition can involve false detection, overgeneralization, premature fixation, omission, contextual misrecognition, causal misrecognition, and mistaken target boundaries.

This paper therefore treats metacognition as:

> **The control system that monitors cognitive output and the way cognition itself is performed, and, when needed, calibrates, suppresses, suspends, updates, and reopens it.**

In engineering terms:

```text
cognition     = sensing / preprocessing system
metacognition = control / audit system
```

This division itself is a current explanatory model. If another observation breaks the cognition/metacognition boundary, the observation is not discarded in order to protect the distinction. The distinction is reconstructed instead.

Cognition generates a world-image. Metacognition prevents that world-image from becoming fixed as the world itself and returns to re-cognition when necessary.

---

# 5. What is Cognitive Engineering?

From the above, this paper defines Cognitive Engineering as follows:

> **Cognitive Engineering is the engineering of the processes by which targets, differences, relations, meanings, values, laws, and related structures are established through cognition from the world, together with the control processes that audit, update, stop, and reopen those cognitive outputs according to purpose.**

Making a UI easier to understand, improving information presentation, or designing a human–machine system can be applications of Cognitive Engineering. None of them alone defines Cognitive Engineering.

The core of Cognitive Engineering handles at least:

- what is cognized as a target,
- which differences are preserved,
- which relations are established,
- what is fixed as a judgment,
- where assertion is stopped,
- what remains unconfirmed or unobserved,
- how far new observation is fed back,
- whether the present cognitive subject, target, relations, and evaluation axes themselves must be reconstructed.

The final item is important. Cognitive Engineering does not merely optimize the inside of an already fixed world. When necessary, it goes back as far as “what was being treated as the world” and redesigns from there.

# 6. Critique of existing Cognitive Engineering

This paper does not reject, as a whole, the local research and technology conducted under the existing label “Cognitive Engineering.”

Its criticism is directed at the classification structure.

Collecting human–machine systems, interfaces, information presentation, work support, error reduction, decision support, and similar work under an undefined concept of cognition does not by itself make the collection Cognitive Engineering.

> **What is being treated as cognition? Where in cognition is the intervention applied? What cognitive state is the target?**

Unless these are shown, “cognitive” is merely a modifier.

This paper therefore criticizes the current field called Cognitive Engineering as follows:

> **Although it contains useful local engineering disciplines, the label “Cognitive Engineering” itself has lost the meaning and value required to identify an engineering target.**

The necessary action is not to abandon Cognitive Engineering. It is to return Cognitive Engineering to its actual target.

However, the phrase “actual target” used here is not treated as a historical entity. It means the target of Cognitive Engineering that this paper currently judges to be most coherent.

# 7. Why Cognitive Engineering is the ancestor of all academic disciplines

When this paper states that “Cognitive Engineering is the ancestor of all academic disciplines,” it is not making a historical claim that a department called Cognitive Engineering existed before physics or mathematics.

The meaning is structural.

Before any academic discipline can exist, there is at least cognition, targeting, distinction, naming, concept retention, relation-building, observation, recording, comparison, judgment, explanation, correction, and transmission.

Before physics treats physical phenomena, there is the act of determining what is carved out as a physical phenomenon. Before mathematics operates on targets, there is the act of determining what counts as identical, what counts as different, and which relations are adopted. Before medicine classifies pathology, there is the act of deciding what will count as normal, abnormal, or a single state.

> **Before any academic discipline begins, there are cognitive operations that establish a world that discipline can handle.**

Cognitive Engineering deals with this preceding stage.

Therefore:

> **Cognitive Engineering is not one branch among all academic disciplines. It is foundational engineering that deals with the stage that enables all academic disciplines to be established.**

This positioning is also the current structural recognition of v0.4. If the divisions among academic disciplines, cognition, and world formation are reconstructed in the future, this position will also be re-audited.

---

# 8. Humanity has always practiced unnamed Cognitive Engineering

Before humanity possessed the name “Cognitive Engineering,” it had already created classification systems, units, symbols, languages, diagrams, mathematics, observation methods, experimental methods, judgment criteria, and divisions among academic disciplines.

All of these are also operations on how the world is cognized, externalized, and made reusable.

Cognitive Engineering is therefore not a newly added special field.

> **It is the activity of directly treating as an engineering target the operations humanity has always performed but has not sufficiently objectified as an independent foundational engineering discipline.**

This is not a linear historical-origin claim. It is a present structural redescription.

---

# 9. Boundaries in Cognitive Engineering

The fact that cognition can be treated as an engineering target does not justify completely dismantling, predicting, fixing, inducing, or controlling cognition.

Cognitive Engineering can directly approach human decision-making, autonomy, dignity, and irreversibility.

Observation boundaries, action boundaries, reversibility, stopping, and preservation of the unobserved and unknown are therefore required.

**神の領域原理（仮）** is positioned as the public principle governing this upper boundary.

The **Trinity Principle** is positioned as the public structural principle governing targets, relations, and closure in cognition-after descriptions.

Both are public principles of the Cognitive Engineering system, but neither exhausts Cognitive Engineering.

# 12. Time axis and reopening

The conclusions of this paper are not static definitions independent of time.

New observations or implementation results may show, for example, that:

- what had been treated as cognition divides into multiple systems,
- the distinction between cognition and metacognition breaks down,
- the boundary between subject and target changes,
- an activity appears that cannot be handled by the current Science/Engineering separation,
- an application result of Cognitive Engineering exposes a defect in the definition of Cognitive Engineering itself.

In such cases, the target of change is not limited to local terminology.

> **The questions, targets, definitions, relations, criticisms, the positioning as “ancestor of all academic disciplines,” and past interpretations in this paper are all reopened.**

The older version is not deleted. It is preserved as a record of the cognitive world at that time.

# 13. Self-application

If Cognitive Engineering treats cognition-based world formation and its audit as engineering targets, then the establishment of Cognitive Engineering itself cannot be exempted from audit.

> **Cognitive Engineering treats its own targeting, definition, academic boundaries, value judgments, and stopping conditions as targets of Cognitive Engineering.**

The moment it exempts only itself as a final definition, it becomes inconsistent with the definition given in this paper.

---

# 14. Non-goals

This paper does not aim to completely explain human cognition, fully determine the activation cause of cognition, fully visualize human decision-making, provide technologies for domination through cognitive manipulation, or reduce all academic disciplines to Cognitive Engineering in a way that denies their distinctiveness.

“Ancestor of all academic disciplines” means that cognitive operations exist before each academic discipline can be established, and Cognitive Engineering treats that prior stage as an engineering target.

---

# 15. Conclusion

> **Cognition comes before academic disciplines.**

Science observes what holds in observable phenomena, actions, and relations. Engineering acts on targets toward intended states.

Cognition itself cannot be directly observed from outside. A cognitive model can be constructed from external observation, but under the current definition of this paper, it is not accepted that promoting that model into cognition itself establishes “Cognitive Science.”

Cognitive Engineering treats as engineering targets the operations that exist before all academic disciplines: how humans cognize something as a world, carve out targets, form relations, make judgments, stop, and update again.

In this sense:

> **Cognitive Engineering is the ancestor of all academic disciplines.**

This conclusion is not promoted into the final hierarchy of the world-itself. This paper itself remains a time-bounded cognition-after Projection: locally closed and globally reopenable.
