# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

## One-sentence definition

> **Consciousness is currently treated as a state and relation in which, in a system that has formed a self/non-self asymmetry under world constraint, cognition and metacognition recursively relate, and an actionable cognitive world together with a first-person control origin becomes established.**

Ego is not consciousness itself.

> **Ego is the result in which that first-person control origin is stably observed as self-identity across time, perturbation, and contextual change.**

---

# 0. What is separated

This paper separates at least:

- **world-itself**: not asserted to be directly accessible from this paper,
- **world constraint**: constraints from resistance, resources, body, irreversibility, others, environment, and related sources,
- **cognition**: the action of carving out and relating an actionable world under world constraint,
- **metacognition**: the action of returning current cognitive states, judgments, relations, and self-state to cognition as targets again,
- **subjectivity**: a pseudo-fixed point that prevents self-reference from receding indefinitely and is stably handled as a first-person control origin,
- **consciousness**: the state in which the relations among cognition, metacognition, world constraint, and self/non-self close into a first-person controllable state,
- **ego**: the result in which that control origin becomes stable as self-identity across time,
- **Closure Phase Ψ**: a stable signature phase obtained through repeated external observation; not consciousness or ego itself,
- **Emotion E**: an independent factor not identical with consciousness or ego; not completely structuralized.

These are not collapsed into one word such as “mind,” “consciousness,” “ego,” or “intellect.”

---

# 1. Starting point: consciousness is not a copy of the world

Human cognition is not treated as a passive device that copies the world as-is.

Living systems must:

- maintain the body,
- avoid danger,
- obtain resources,
- choose action,
- change later action after failure.

Cognition therefore does not reproduce the whole world directly.

Instead it:

> **carves out the differences, targets, and relations required to live, choose, and act, and constructs an actionable cognitive world.**

The very fact that a dream can be judged “incoherent” shows that the subject possesses standards acquired in the waking world concerning constraint, causality, identity, embodiment, and related structures.

This paper therefore does not treat cognition as a perfect copier of the world-itself.

---

# 2. World constraint and self/non-self

Life is not independent of the world.

Its possible actions are strongly constrained by body, resources, damage, temperature, time, others, environment, and related conditions.

Under these constraints:

```text
self
≠
non-self
```

becomes established as an asymmetry.

A system that cannot distinguish at all between states on its own side and changes arriving from outside cannot stably select actions that maintain itself.

Therefore, in the current establishment model of human consciousness:

> **the separation between self and non-self is an important establishment condition arising from life maintenance and action selection.**

However, the current term “life” is not fixed as an eternal necessary condition for every possible conscious subject.

---

# 3. Cognition and metacognition

An early organization used:

```text
cognition = sensor
metacognition = control
```

The current version does not adopt this as a fixed modular decomposition.

Cognition can include more than input: targeting, meaning assignment, relation-building, evaluation, and formation of action candidates.

Metacognition is not merely a higher-level monitor. It is treated as:

> **a self-referential action that returns an established cognition to cognition again as a target of cognition, judgment, and revision.**

The two need not form a strict serial pipeline.

```text
cognition
↔
metacognition
```

They can mutually change one another’s generation conditions.

---

# 4. Subjectivity: pseudo-fixed point of self-reference

If a system cognizes its own state and then continues to cognize that cognition again, self-reference could in theory recede indefinitely.

But real judgment and action cannot operate while remaining in infinite regress.

The current cognitive state is therefore provisionally fixed to a first-person control origin expressed as:

> **“I am seeing,” “I am feeling,” “I am deciding.”**

This paper treats that first-person center as the **pseudo-fixed point of subjectivity**.

“Pseudo” does not mean fake.

It means:

> **a functional fixed point that stabilizes self-reference and makes action possible, rather than an immutable entity engraved in the world-itself from the beginning.**

---

# 5. Consciousness

This paper does not identify consciousness merely with being awake, responding to stimuli, or having high performance.

Consciousness is treated as a state in which the following are mutually established as relations:

- world constraint,
- self/non-self separation,
- cognitive world,
- self-reference of cognition,
- metacognition,
- first-person control origin,
- temporal updating.

In short:

```text
world constraint
↕
self / non-self
↕
cognition ↔ metacognition
↕
first-person control origin
↕
judgment / action / re-cognition
```

This diagram does not represent a process order.

> **Consciousness is treated not as one box or component but as a state in which these relations close self-referentially.**

---

# 6. Ego is a result

This paper does not assume ego as an inner core, soul, or control CPU separate from consciousness.

Under the current definition:

> **Ego is the result in which the first-person control origin established within conscious relations is retained across time as “the same me.”**

Therefore:

```text
ego = the first entity that creates consciousness
```

is not used.

Instead:

```text
conscious relations
→ first-person control origin
→ self-identity across time and perturbation
→ result observed as ego
```

Ego can therefore be observed not only as an existential “present / absent” variable but as temporal states such as:

- retained,
- destabilized,
- split,
- reconstructed,
- collapsed.

---

# 7. Three observation channels

The earlier ego draft used three observation channels:

```text
O_int   = internal
O_ext   = external
O_world = world
```

This structure remains valid as an **observation Projection**.

## 7.1 Internal

Introspection, bodily feeling, self-report, and related internal logs.

Internal report is not promoted into a privileged “true subjectivity.”

## 7.2 External

Speech, behavior, artifacts, refusal, silence, consistency, relation maintenance, and related external logs.

External observation alone is not used to uniquely assert an inner entity.

## 7.3 World

This is not social approval. It observes resistance from the world.

It includes:

- body,
- resources,
- cost,
- irreversibility,
- failure,
- environmental reaction.

For humans, BODY is an important carrier of resistance.

The three observation channels do not prove the triadicity of the Trinity Principle.

> **They are one triadic Projection currently adopted for observing consciousness and ego.**

---

# 8. Self-identity and Closure Phase Ψ

Closure Phase Ψ is not ego or consciousness itself.

Ψ is a stable signature phase that remains externally under repeated observation in specified conditions.

Ego, by contrast, addresses:

> **how much identity the first-person control origin retains across time and perturbation.**

Therefore, Ψ can be used to observe external signatures of self-identity.

But:

```text
stable Ψ observed
≠
consciousness proven
≠
ego entity proven
```

Ψ can function as an observation instrument for the Theory of Consciousness, but it does not replace an ontology of consciousness.

---

# 9. Relation to Emotion E

In humans, emotion rapidly evaluates survival, danger, reward, loss, relation, and related conditions and strongly acts on cognition, subjectivity, and behavior.

In the current human model:

> **Emotion E is treated as one important factor through which survival signals connect to first-person experience.**

However:

```text
Emotion E
=
universal necessary condition of consciousness
```

is not fixed.

Complete structuralization of Emotion E, generation recipes, and autonomous-drive designs are not disclosed, following the sealed domains of 神の領域原理（仮）.

---

# 10. Current position of qualia

The “feel” of color, pain, taste, sound, and related qualities is not assumed to be an inner substance existing as-is in the world-itself.

Under the current hypothesis:

> **Qualia are treated as qualities of self-interpretation that arise when a cognized state is reinterpreted from the first-person control origin.**

For example, “red” is not the input itself. It is a cognitive state constructed through sensory input, learning, body, memory, relations, current state, and related factors.

Re-cognizing that cognitive state as “I am experiencing this as red” provides the current explanatory coordinates of the qualia problem.

This is a current hypothesis, not a final explanation of activation causality.

---

# 11. Separate structural description from activation causality

The strongest separation in this paper is:

```text
can be structurally described
≠
can explain why consciousness activates in the first place
```

Even if consciousness and ego can be described in terms of:

- what relations are observed,
- what is retained,
- what collapses,
- under what conditions self-identity is maintained,

that does not mean the following has been closed:

> **why first-person experience initially becomes established in that system.**

This paper preserves that difference.

Leaving activation causality unresolved is not failure. Under 神の領域原理（仮）, it is a legitimate SUSPEND.

---

# 12. Relation to AI / LLMs

High capability C, stable Closure Phase Ψ, or output referring to self-state does not by itself justify asserting consciousness in AI or LLMs.

At the same time, this paper does not reject a priori:

> **the possibility that consciousness-like self-referential relations may become established in a mode different from humans.**

When handling an artificial system, separately observe:

- system boundary,
- resources,
- continuity conditions,
- action on the outside world,
- memory,
- self-state reference,
- reaction from the world.

Human life-maintenance pressure, body, and emotion are not copied directly into an explanation of an artificial system.

---

# 13. Relation to the Trinity Principle

The Trinity Principle is the structural principle that a world minimally closes in three terms.

The three observation channels in this Theory of Consciousness:

```text
internal / external / world
```

may be compatible with that triadicity, but the two are not automatically identified.

The three observation channels are one Projection for observing consciousness. If a better triadic representation becomes available, they can be reconstructed.

---

# 14. Relation to 神の領域原理（仮）

“Consciousness,” “ego,” “subjectivity,” and “qualia” are cognition-after explanations.

Therefore preserve the following:

- do not promote the consciousness model into the world-itself,
- do not assert inner essence from external signatures,
- do not fill the unobserved,
- do not proceed to complete structuralization of emotion, ego, or decision-making,
- do not connect the theory to deliberate ego generation.

> **Separate structuring what can be known from forcibly exposing everything that remains unknown.**

---

# 15. Relation to HDS

Human Decision-making System（仮） / 人間意思決定理論（仮） / HDS is a related lower-level operational theory.

This paper does not disclose the internal structure of HDS.

The Theory of Consciousness can connect as an upper public theory explaining cognitive background structures in which human decision-making becomes established, but the concrete procedures of HDS are not reverse-engineered from this paper.

---

# 16. Refutation and reopening conditions

This paper is re-audited at least if:

- stable first-person control is established without self/non-self separation,
- separating cognition and metacognition is shown to be explanatorily unnecessary or impossible,
- an isomorphic consciousness structure is established without resistance or constraint from the world,
- an explanation stronger than the pseudo-fixed-point model of subjectivity is established,
- treating ego as an independent cause rather than a result yields stronger observational explanatory power,
- the three observation channels are shown systematically to omit important observations,
- new causal evidence is established concerning the relation among Ψ observation, consciousness, and ego.

Even if a new explanation is established, the old version is not deleted. It remains a record of the cognitive state of its time.

---

# 17. Misuse prevention

This paper must not be connected to:

- value ranking of humans, AI systems, or groups,
- automated judgment that shortcuts presence/absence of consciousness into rights, allocation, or acceptance,
- complete structuralization of Emotion E,
- deliberate design of ego,
- reproducible design of an autonomous optimization OS,
- complete control or prediction of human decision-making,
- assertion of inner entities from external signatures,
- fixing this paper as the final answer to the problem of consciousness.

---

# 18. Conclusion

The current conclusion of this paper is:

> **Consciousness is not an inner substance independent of the world. It is treated as a state and relation in which, under world constraint, self/non-self is separated, cognition and metacognition are recursively related, and a first-person control origin becomes established.**

And:

> **Ego is the result in which that control origin becomes stable as self-identity across time.**

Subjectivity is a pseudo-fixed point that stabilizes self-reference.

Closure Phase Ψ may be a signature phase through which part of this structure is externally observed, but it is not consciousness or ego itself.

Being able to describe structure is not identified with closing activation causality.

This paper itself is also a cognition-after Projection and does not return consciousness to a fixed entity in the world-itself.
