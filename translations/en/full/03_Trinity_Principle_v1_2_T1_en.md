# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

## One-sentence definition

> **The Trinity Principle is the principle that a world constructed and operated after cognition closes in three, and that a triad is the minimum structure of world construction.**

Its shortest form is:

```text
The world closes in three.
The elements that construct a world are three.
Three is the minimum structure.
```

“Three” here does not mean numerology, religious symbolism, or the number of physical objects.

It means:

> **the minimum number of structural roles required for a world to close as a world and become operable.**

---

# 0. Separation between the primitive principle and the current Projection

The upstream proposition of the Trinity Principle is triadicity itself.

The later formulation:

```text
W := (X, R, M)
```

is a representative Projection for bringing this principle down into concrete world-description, audit, and implementation.

Therefore:

```text
Trinity Principle
≠
the literal string X / R / M
```

At the same time, triadicity is not weakened into “merely a currently convenient notation.” This version **strongly adopts as a principle claim the proposition that three itself is the minimum structure of world closure**.

What can be reopened is not limited to term names or role decompositions such as A/B/C or X/R/M. If a counterexample to triadic minimality itself is established, the current version of the Trinity Principle is re-audited.

---

# 1. What “world” means in this paper

Following the boundary of **神の領域原理（仮）**, this paper does not directly assert the number of physical constituents of the pre-cognitive world-itself.

A “world” in this paper means:

> **a cognitive world constructed when an observer carves something out, establishes differences, relations, and boundaries, and turns it into a unit within which judgment, description, action, and re-cognition become possible.**

Therefore, the proposition:

```text
the world closes in three
```

states the minimum closure structure by which something becomes established and operable as a cognition-after world.

It is not the proposition that “the pre-cognitive world-itself is physically made of three parts.”

---

# 2. Why three?

## 2.1 One does not close

With only one term, it is not established what distinguishes that one term from anything else, what it relates to, or where its identity boundary lies.

If differences, relations, or rules are embedded inside the one term, then the term already contains multiple functional roles and is not a pure one-term closure.

One term can therefore indicate existence, a target, or a state, but it is insufficient as the minimum structure of world closure.

## 2.2 Two creates relation but does not close

With two terms, difference, contrast, direction, interaction, and relation can be established.

But with only two terms, the following can remain outside the pair:

- how the two should be read,
- which relation is valid,
- where identity and distinction are bounded,
- under what conditions the structure counts as established, failed, or stopped.

When two terms appear sufficient to close the world, the audit checks whether a reading rule, boundary, rule, context, observation position, or similar condition has already been shared and is functioning as an invisible third condition.

## 2.3 Three closes

With a third term, the difference and relation between the first two can themselves be closed through another condition, relation, or boundary.

Abstractly:

```text
A
B
C
```

can mutually supply their conditions of establishment and bring the previously externalized reading, boundary, and relation back inside the world.

Therefore:

> **With three terms, for the first time, a world containing target, difference, relation, and boundary minimally closes.**

## 2.4 “Three” is a number of structural roles, not a number of objects

Triadicity does not mean that three physical objects are required.

Three functional roles may exist inside one target, or many targets may be projected into three structural roles.

What matters is that **a minimum of three independent structural roles required for closure become established**.

## 2.5 Four or more

Four or more elements, states, variables, or roles can of course exist.

The claim of this version is:

> **Three is the minimum structure that first establishes the qualitative transition called closure; four or more are not required for minimality.**

Four or more may appear as division, detailing, composition, parallelization, recursion, history, or additional state within triadic structure.

If a world is demonstrated in which one and two do not close, three also does not close, and **an independent fourth structural role is required in principle for closure**, that becomes a valid candidate counterexample to the triadic minimality of this version.

# 3. Representative operational Projection: X / R / M

As the representative Projection for explicitly handling triadicity in the current cognitive world, this paper adopts:

```text
W_t := (X_t, R_t, M_t)
```

## 3.1 X: target

`X_t` is the role of carving out “what is being handled” at that time.

It may include targets, identifiers, versions, settings, observation range, time range, state, and related items.

## 3.2 R: relation

`R_t` is the role of handling “how the targets relate” at that time.

It may include comparison, transition, dependency, relations treated as causal, input/output, observation procedures, perturbation conditions, and related structures.

## 3.3 M: closure condition / meta-rule

`M_t` is the role of handling “how that relation is read and where it closes” at that time.

It may include identity, boundaries, judgment, stopping, logs, refutation conditions, use boundaries, and related rules.

## 3.4 X / R / M is not the only possible triad

```text
triadicity = principle
X / R / M = current representative operational Projection
```

If another triadic representation projects the same minimum closure structure more faithfully, it may become a candidate for adoption.

However, if changing the names of the terms also loses triadicity itself, the system must audit whether the new representation is another expression of the same principle or a different principle.

---

# 4. Relation operation

A triad is not three static boxes.

Acquiring a target, finding a difference, forming a relation, converting something into another representation, drawing a boundary, or projecting a result into the next world all involve relation operation.

Through relation operation, not only can the content of one of the three terms change; **what is carved out as the three terms in the first place can also change**.

The Trinity Principle is therefore not a principle that fixes A, B, and C once and forever.

---

# 5. Asymmetric circulation

A world closed by three terms acts, through the result of closure, on the next observation, judgment, relation, and boundary.

```text
triadic closure_t
→ output / action / observation
→ conditions for the next world construction
→ triadic closure_(t+1)
```

The returned world need not be identical to the world before the cycle began.

This is because:

- observation can change the meaning of the target,
- judgment can change relations,
- execution can change the environment,
- description can change the observer.

> **It returns, but it does not return to the same place.**

This is treated as asymmetric circulation.

---

# 6. Asymmetric recursion

The result of one triadic closure can again be treated as a target, relation, or condition of the next triadic closure.

In other words, triadic structure can recurse.

However, identity of target, relation, meaning, observer, boundary, and time before and after recursion is not assumed.

```text
T_t
→ the next world containing T_t
→ T_(t+1)
```

Even when self-reference or recursion occurs, the structure does not return to a perfectly identical circle. It moves to a new phase while retaining history.

This is asymmetric recursion.

---

# 7. Local closure and global reopening

The fact that a world closes in three and the claim that the whole world becomes permanently and finally closed are different.

```text
a local world closes in three
≠
the whole world and all future time close permanently in one pass
```

A world required for a current judgment, implementation, or description may be strongly closed as a triadic structure.

But new observation, the passage of time, or action results may reopen even:

- what counts as one term,
- the division of roles among the three terms,
- the world boundary,
- the observer,
- the relation,
- the closure condition,
- the explanation of triadicity itself.

Closure is not termination. It is a locally established point that enables the next reopening.

---

# 8. SUSPEND

SUSPEND is not itself the primitive substance of the Trinity Principle. It is an important stopping output derived when triadic closure is operated.

If one of the three terms, or the relation by which the three are established, remains unresolved and the world has not yet closed, assertion is not forced.

- **ASSERT**: the current world is sufficiently closed for the required range and can be asserted
- **SUSPEND**: unobserved, undefined, unclosed, or undecidable
- **REJECT**: unacceptable under specification or definition
- **OUT-OF-SCOPE**: outside use, disclosure, or danger boundaries
- **FAIL**: operational failure such as Runtime failure

Silence and reservation can be states that **correctly preserve the fact that the world has not yet closed**, rather than information loss.

---

# 9. Relation to 神の領域原理（仮）

The roles of the two principles differ.

```text
Trinity Principle
→ What is the minimum structure by which a world is established and closes?
→ Three is the minimum structure.

神の領域原理（仮）
→ Is that closed cognition-after world being misprojected into the pre-cognitive world-itself?
→ How are invisible phase difference, danger boundaries, sealing, and stopping handled?
```

Therefore, “the world closes in three” in the Trinity Principle does not cross the boundary of 神の領域原理（仮） and assert the physical tripartition of the world-itself.

Conversely, the total provisionality of 神の領域原理（仮） is not used to weaken the triadic minimality of the Trinity Principle into “just an arbitrary notation.”

---

# 10. Relation to Closure Phase Ψ

Closure Phase Ψ addresses stable signatures that appear externally under repeated observation in fixed conditions.

The Trinity Principle asks what minimum triadic structure closes the observation world in which that signature is observed.

X / R / M is a representative operational Projection usable for that purpose.

The successful observation of Ψ is neither an ontological proof of triadicity nor a unique identification of its internal cause.

---

# 11. Relation to HDS

Human Decision-making System（仮） / 人間意思決定理論（仮） / HDS is a related lower-level operational theory.

This paper discloses only the names and conceptual relation. It does not disclose the internal structure of HDS, action connections, evaluation design, implementation, or reproducible operating procedures.

The asymmetric circulation and asymmetric recursion of the Trinity Principle may connect to operational theories such as HDS, but this paper does not reverse-engineer HDS from the public principle.

---

# 12. Self-application

The Trinity Principle itself is not exempt from the audit of triadicity.

This version explicitly states:

- principle claim: the world closes in three,
- minimality claim: three is the minimum structure of world construction,
- representative Projection: X / R / M,
- dynamic extensions: asymmetric circulation and asymmetric recursion,
- boundary: do not promote a cognition-after world into the world-itself,
- reopening condition: a valid counterexample to triadic minimality.

If explaining this principle always requires an implicit fourth term outside the triad, and that fourth term cannot be brought back inside the triad as relation, then this version has a self-application problem.

---

# 13. Refutation and reopening conditions

At least the following are conditions for re-auditing this version:

- one term alone closes a world without implicit relation, boundary, or rule,
- two terms alone close meaning, boundary, and judgment without sharing or hiding a third condition,
- a world is shown that does not close in principle with three terms and requires an independent fourth structure,
- another triadic Projection stronger than X / R / M is established,
- counting in terms of triads is shown to omit relation operation itself,
- the explanation of asymmetric circulation or asymmetric recursion contradicts triadic minimality.

The establishment of another triadic Projection is not, by itself, a refutation of the Trinity Principle.

A **counterexample that breaks triadic minimality itself** and a **representational limitation of X / R / M** are treated separately.

---

# 14. Misuse prevention

This paper must not be connected to:

- mystifying the number three or granting it religious authority,
- ranking humans, personalities, or groups,
- declaring that anything not representable as a triad does not exist,
- fixing X / R / M as the one absolute triad,
- promoting local closure into global final closure,
- asserting a cognition-after world as the pre-cognitive world-itself,
- using closure as an authority shield to silence others.

---

# 15. Conclusion

The first primitive form of the Trinity Principle is simple:

> **The world closes in three.**

> **The elements that construct a world are three.**

> **Three is the minimum structure.**

X / R / M is the representative Projection that makes this triadicity operable.

Triadic closure is not static. It circulates asymmetrically by returning action results into the conditions of the next world construction, and it recurses asymmetrically by making the result of closure a target again.

A local world closes. Across time, the whole remains reopenable.

And this principle itself is no exception.
