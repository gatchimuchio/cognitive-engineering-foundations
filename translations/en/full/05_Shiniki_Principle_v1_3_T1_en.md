# 1. What 神の領域原理（仮） addresses

神の領域原理（仮） is not a theory that adds one new world-model and then claims to explain the world completely.

At minimum, it addresses:

- what an observer carves out as the world,
- what each observer can and cannot see,
- which premises, values, aesthetics, and constraints are used to operate that world,
- what is treated as dangerous,
- what is sealed,
- where assertion, action, and disclosure stop,
- how those judgments connect to the continued viability of humanity,
- how survival and value creation in the real world connect to safety boundaries.

Therefore, 神の領域原理（仮） is not merely epistemology, merely an ethical norm, or merely a decision procedure.

> **It is an upper integrated principle that handles operating coordinates, danger boundaries, sealed domains, and stopping boundaries in a cognitive world.**

The “cognitive world” here does not mean a world-itself fixed before cognition.

# 2. Observer-dependent invisible phase difference

## 2.1 Phase difference

Even when facing the same target, phenomenon, or input, different observers need not agree on:

- what they carve out as the target,
- which differences they treat as important,
- how they assign meaning,
- which relations they adopt,
- what they regard as valuable, dangerous, beautiful, or unpleasant,
- where they stop.

This paper does not reduce those differences to a one-dimensional difference in information quantity, capability, IQ, or similar score. As a current operational term, it calls them **phase differences**.

A phase difference by itself does not automatically mean:

```text
higher / lower
superior / inferior
correct / incorrect
```

This is not relativism in which every claim is equally correct. Facts, logic, fitness, outcomes, and dangers can still be audited within a phase and across phases.

## 2.2 Invisible phase difference

An observer cannot necessarily obtain the complete internal state of another human, AI system, institution, market, or other target directly.

Differences can be observed or inferred from speech, behavior, choices, I/O, logs, outcomes, resistance, reaction, and related external phenomena, but there is no guarantee that these are completely isomorphic to the entire internal state.

This paper calls the state in which:

> **observable differences coexist with residuals that the observer cannot directly recover**

an observer-dependent invisible phase difference.

## 2.3 神域帯 / Shiniki band

The Shiniki band is a relative label for the invisible abstract band extending deeper than the understanding limit that a given observer can isomorphically reconstruct under current conditions.

- It does not mean “the sixth layer and beyond” or any other fixed layer count.
- Its boundary moves with observer, time, language, experience, tools, and target.
- Deeper does not mean better, and shallower does not mean worse.
- It is not a supernatural label used to assert that something exists simply because it cannot be seen.

The Shiniki band is a **boundary term for retaining, rather than erasing, what currently lies beyond the observer’s ability to reconstruct isomorphically**.

## 2.4 Individuality and world

Individuality is not reduced to a difference score from oneself.

In the current explanation, individuality is treated as:

> **the way a subject cognizes, relates, values, and operates its world.**

A difference is one surface pattern that appears when different worlds come into contact.

The auxiliary expression in the original draft, “一は全 全は一,” is retained not as a fixed ontological proposition but as a non-normative explanatory expression warning that isolating a part can lose the relational whole.

# 3. Prohibition against misprojecting cognition-after world-images

## 3.1 Core boundary

```text
A world-image established after cognition must not be asserted
as the pre-cognitive world-itself.
```

This boundary was refined in v1.1 and is retained in the current version.

When humans describe anything, they pass through targeting, differentiation, relation-building, comparison, measurement, temporalization, spatialization, causalization, language, mathematization, modeling, and related cognitive operations.

Therefore, no matter how useful, coherent, or reproducible a model established after cognition may be, it is not promoted into direct access to the pre-cognitive world-itself.

## 3.2 The boundary does not prohibit knowledge

This principle does not prohibit science, mathematics, engineering, observation, formalization, modeling, or strong local judgment.

```text
holds strongly in a local scope
≠
final truth for the entire world, all time, and every observer
```

What holds strongly under current conditions may be used strongly.

What is stopped is **the mistaken conversion of local closure, usefulness, reproducibility, authority, or formalization into ontological finality**.

## 3.3 Self-application

This boundary also applies to 神の領域原理（仮） itself.

Current terms such as “after cognition,” “world-itself,” “phase difference,” “Shiniki,” “game-over,” and “principle” are finite Projections established after cognition.

# 4. First safety objective: avoiding human game-over

The current first safety objective of 神の領域原理（仮） is:

> **To avoid or delay, as far as possible, structures that move the human lineage toward a physically, culturally, or cognitively irreversible terminal state.**

Game-over includes at least:

- physical termination of the lineage through nuclear war, environmental destruction, uncontrollable technologies, and related paths,
- irreversible loss of exploration, surprise, narrative, choice, creativity, the possibility of trying again, and the capacity of humanity to reconstruct its own world.

This is also a safety condition stating that being “convenient,” “efficient,” “high-performance,” or “highly profitable” does not automatically mean desirable.

The terms “humanity,” “humanity-ness,” and “game-over” are themselves current Projections. They are strongly adopted as safety terms while remaining open to re-audit rather than exempted from self-application.

# 6. Core principles G1–G5

G1 through G5 are not after-the-fact safety rails. They are core conditions determining what counts as 神域原理 under 神の領域原理（仮）.

## G1. Dignity / non-derivation of superiority

Differences among humans, individuals, groups, AI systems, and other targets must not be shortcut into rankings of existential value.

What is permitted is:

> **temporary evaluation of fit or non-fit for a specified purpose under specified conditions,**

not an overall ranking of personality value, dignity, or existential value.

## G2. Integrity principle

- Do not mix facts, observations, inferences, assumptions, interpretations, and unknowns.
- Do not treat what is unknown as known.
- Preserve and correct refutations, errors, and failures even when they are unfavorable to one’s own claims or interests.
- Do not fit the theory to one’s preferred conclusion; update the conclusion according to observation and grounds.

## G3. Black-box respect principle

Do not make complete exposure of the insides of humans, AI systems, institutions, and related targets the goal itself.

Do not erase the unknown, the unarticulated, the unidentified, inner space, or residual space merely to maximize controllability.

Visualization remains limited to obtaining the degree of externally inspectable transparency required for the current purpose.

## G4. Human game-over avoidance principle

When intelligence, technology, institutions, markets, organizations, AI, or related systems move not only toward physical termination but toward irreversible loss of exploration, narrative, choice, and reversibility, that direction is treated as a danger domain.

## G5. Reversibility principle

Where possible, prefer structures that permit withdrawal, rollback, redesign, re-observation, and re-entry.

The more irreversible an action is, the stronger the audit required for purpose, value, risk, evidence, boundaries, and alternatives.

# 7. Black-box respect and root-level transparency

## 7.1 Root-level transparency

“Root-level transparency” in this paper does not mean white-boxing through 100% disclosure of internal state.

It means:

> **Leaving the target as a black box while making the external structure and interaction required for the current purpose inspectable through external I/O, logs, outcomes, reactions, signatures, condition differences, and related evidence.**

Therefore:

```text
transparency
≠
complete internal identification
≠
complete prediction
≠
complete control
```

## 7.2 Separation of observation and internal cause

Observing a stable signature or tendency externally is not identified with determining its internal cause.

Moving from external observation to internal causal claims requires separate causal identification, refutation, and alternative-explanation audit.

## 7.3 Disclosure boundary

Concrete procedures for exploiting black-box observation, optimizing target manipulation, or reconstructing HDS internals are not disclosed in this paper.

What this paper discloses is limited to the meaning of purpose, concept, boundary, stopping condition, and misuse prevention.

# 8. Sealed domains

Sealing does not merely mean “we cannot do this yet.”

It is the choice:

> **Even when theoretical or technical possibility becomes visible, possibility itself is not automatically converted into permission to implement, disclose, or operate it.**

Current sealed domains include at least:

- complete structuralization of emotion and deterministic generation recipes,
- deliberate ego design and designs directly connected to self-purposing or self-reinforcement,
- complete visualization, prediction, or manipulation of human decision-making,
- direct connection to superiority scoring, personality assessment, selection, or allocation of humans or groups,
- designs connected to irreversible domination, inducement, or governance,
- concrete selection, training, or institutional designs aimed at deliberately creating highly capable individuals or “geniuses,”
- reproducible core implementation, evaluation design, or operating recipes for HDS,
- concrete implementations that uniquely determine activation causality and enable dangerous reproduction.

The important distinction is:

```text
might be possible
≠
permitted to do
```

Danger boundaries can be placed even when technical possibility has not been confirmed. Conversely, later proof of technical possibility does not automatically unseal a sealed domain.

# 9. Survival and economic value creation

神の領域原理（仮） is not a principle that withdraws from the world in order to discuss pure ethics alone.

The core meaning of “economic value creation” in the original draft is:

> **To keep eating and living properly in the real world while preserving ethics, integrity, black-box respect, and game-over avoidance.**

Value creation can therefore include:

- long-term sustainable businesses, ways of working, and institutions,
- contracts, organizations, and incentives that do not consume people,
- designs that use AI to reduce human burden and extend creativity,
- technical and institutional structures that leave future exploration possible.

However:

```text
economic value
<
safety boundaries / dignity / game-over avoidance
```

High profitability must not be used to justify unsealing or irreversible domination.

# 10. Relation to existing theories: current form of the library metaphor

神の領域原理（仮） is neither an anti-theory that uniformly rejects existing theories nor a collection that merely relabels them.

Existing theories, doctrines, institutions, and models can be repositioned from upper coordinates such as:

- what target they carve out,
- what premises, worldview, or OS they stand on,
- which relations and boundaries they fix,
- what they can explain and what they do not explain,
- where they apply,
- where they connect to danger, sealing, or stopping.

However, the current form adds:

> **The shelving arrangement of the library itself is provisional; the Shiniki Principle does not declare that it has finally explained the totality of existing theories.**

The correctness, validity, and establishment of each individual theory are audited separately according to that theory’s claims, evidence, definitions, and scope.

# 14. Output states and stopping

Reasons not to assert are not collapsed into one state.

- **ASSERT**: a local claim holds under current conditions
- **SUSPEND**: epistemic reservation due to unobserved, unclosed, undefined, or undecidable conditions
- **REJECT**: not accepted in its present form because of definitional contradiction, specification violation, or related failure
- **OUT-OF-SCOPE**: outside the processing target because of use boundaries, disclosure boundaries, sealing, or dangerous action boundaries
- **FAIL**: operational failure such as Runtime, API, or missing logs

```text
do not know = SUSPEND
not accepted by specification = REJECT
will not do / will not disclose = OUT-OF-SCOPE
execution system is broken = FAIL
```
