> **Position**: This paper does not deny natural selection, adaptation, inheritance, or evolution as phenomena. From Cognitive Engineering, it audits whether “a theory that explains evolution” is being identified with “the most upstream explanation of the fact that life is living,” and further addresses **thought contamination**, in which a downstream explanatory system constrains perception, question formation, causal interpretation, and evaluation standards themselves.

> **Terminology rule**: “Darwin's Error” in this paper first refers to **the crossing of explanatory levels in which the downstream evolutionary mechanism of natural selection is promoted into the most upstream principle of life itself**. Second, it assigns **Cognitive-Engineering responsibility to the proposer of a theory** when that theory can become an upstream constraint on later perception, question formation, causality, and evaluation, yet its upstream boundary is not sufficiently separated. This paper does not claim that Darwin intended every later confusion, but absence of intent is not treated as erasure of responsibility.

# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

# 1. What is accepted and what is questioned

Evolutionary theory addresses variation across generations, inheritance, selection, reproductive success, population change, and related processes.

Natural selection is a powerful framework for explaining how heritable variation that creates differences in survival and reproduction can be retained or changed within populations.

This paper does not deny that.

The problem is the leap:

```text
can explain evolution
↓
therefore has explained why life lives
```

---

# 2. Separate upstream and downstream

Under the current cognition of this paper, at least the following are separated:

```text
life is living
↓
an individual maintains itself
↓
survival / action / environmental response
↓
reproduction
↓
generational difference
↓
natural selection
↓
adaptation
↓
evolution
```

This is not claimed to be the one and only biological process chart.

What matters is the explanatory hierarchy:

> **Do not place evolution, adaptation, or reproduction upstream of the state/process that “life is living.”**

Evolution explains change occurring in populations of living systems.

It presupposes a world in which life is already alive, individuals are maintained, and generations continue.

---

# 3. “Life lives in order to live”

The most upstream expression in this paper is:

> **Life lives in order to live.**

This is not teleology introducing an external purpose.

It does not claim:

```text
life has a purpose assigned by the universe
```

It claims the opposite.

> **As long as it is life, it continues the state/process called living. A question that requires an external purpose beyond that is itself adding an explanatory level outside life without justification.**

Thus “life lives in order to live” is neither a predictive model nor an evolutionary mechanism.

It is:

> **a boundary sentence for auditing whether the question “why does life live?” is silently demanding a purpose outside life itself.**

---

# 4. Do not identify living with “what survives remains”

“Living” and “what survived remains” are not the same sentence.

```text
life is living
```

refers to an ongoing state/process.

By contrast:

```text
variants that survive and reproduce remain in the population
```

refers to the result of selection.

If the latter is used to explain the former, the explanation easily slides into self-reference:

> **Life lives because what survived survived.**

Natural selection can explain what remains within a population.

That explanation must not be promoted directly into the answer to “why does life live?”

---

# 5. “Darwin's Error”

What this paper calls “Darwin's Error” is:

> **promoting downstream results or mechanisms—adaptation, reproduction, natural selection—into the most upstream cause, purpose, or reason for the existence of life.**

This is not a denial of the theory of natural selection itself.

Separate:

```text
natural selection explains evolution
```

from:

```text
natural selection explains why life lives
```

The former is a biological question.

The latter crosses explanatory levels.

---

# 5.1 Thought contamination

In this paper, **thought contamination** does not mean merely learning incorrect knowledge.

It means:

> **a state in which a downstream explanatory system is promoted beyond its proper scope into an upstream premise, that premise constrains perception, question formation, causality, evaluation, and methods of refutation themselves, and the fact of that constraint becomes difficult to see.**

For evolutionary theory, this paper separates at least five forms.

## 5.1.1 Contamination of perception

At the moment life is considered, an explanatory frame such as:

```text
life
→ evolution
→ adaptation
→ survival / reproduction
```

is silently installed as an initial condition, and **a cognitive path for looking at life itself without first passing through evolutionary theory is closed from the outset**.

The problem is not knowing evolutionary theory.

The problem is:

> **evolutionary theory being embedded as a premise for seeing the world before it is used as one explanatory candidate.**

## 5.1.2 Contamination of questions

The upstream question:

```text
Why is life living?
```

can be automatically transformed into downstream purposes or mechanisms such as:

```text
in order to evolve
in order to adapt
in order to reproduce
in order to leave genes
```

At that point, not only the answer but **the question itself has been transformed into a form internal to evolutionary theory**.

The thinker may therefore not have “answered through evolutionary theory” at all.

> **The question may first have been rewritten into a form that evolutionary theory can answer.**

## 5.1.3 Contamination of causality

In the current hierarchy of this paper:

```text
life is living
↓
generations continue
↓
selection / adaptation / evolution are observed
```

But when explanatory levels are inverted, it is read as if:

```text
life is living
in order to evolve / adapt
```

> **Do not identify “evolution occurs because life exists” with “life exists for evolution.”**

Promoting downstream mechanisms or outcomes back into upstream activation causes or reasons for existence is treated here as contamination of causality.

## 5.1.4 Contamination of evaluation

When evolutionary theory becomes a broad and powerful premise, new explanations, hypotheses, or questions about life can be judged primarily by:

> **whether they are consistent with evolutionary theory.**

Consistency checking inside the proper scope is legitimate.

The problem arises when:

```text
cannot be explained by evolutionary theory
↓
therefore inferior / wrong as an explanation of life
```

is used as a shortcut, while **the scope, premises, and question-form of evolutionary theory itself are removed from the audit target**.

At this point, the theory is not merely evaluating the target.

> **It controls the evaluator itself: what counts as a valid question, explanation, or piece of evidence.**

## 5.1.5 Reproduction

When contamination of perception, questions, causality, and evaluation is repeated through education, textbooks, public discourse, academic practice, and standard explanatory templates, it becomes an initial premise for the next generation.

```text
theory
↓
standard explanation
↓
education / discourse
↓
tacit premise
↓
next question
↓
evaluated by the same theory
↓
reproduction
```

The most serious state is:

> **the very thinking that should be capable of doubting the established explanation becomes able to operate only inside that explanatory system.**

This is deeper than a simple knowledge error.

A wrong answer can be corrected.

But when **the cognitive apparatus that determines what to ask, what counts as a cause, and what counts as refutation is itself fixed**, even the route by which error can be discovered becomes narrower.

---

# 5.2 Using evolutionary theory is not the same as thought contamination

This paper does not call every use of evolutionary theory “contamination.”

Using evolutionary theory as a strong explanatory model for natural selection, adaptation, population change, and related phenomena inside its proper scope is not the problem.

Thought contamination is established when the theory:

- crosses its scope,
- promotes a downstream explanation into an upstream principle,
- reshapes the question to fit itself,
- rejects contrary hypotheses merely because they are inconsistent with the theory,
- stops audit of its own premises,
- and reproduces that stopped audit through education and discourse.

Therefore:

```text
using evolutionary theory
≠
thought contamination
```

The boundary is:

> **Is evolutionary theory being used as an explanatory tool, or has it become an upstream premise that controls what can be thought in the first place?**

---

# 5.3 Responsibility assigned to Darwin

This paper strongly criticizes Darwin not because every individual mechanism of natural selection is “entirely wrong.”

The reason is:

> **A person who proposes an explanatory system powerful enough to become an upstream premise for how later generations perceive life, formulate questions, assign causes, and evaluate explanations bears responsibility when a mistake in scope can constrain later thought for a long period.**

This paper does not make the historical claim that Darwin:

- intended every later misreading,
- directly designed modern education or academic practice,
- fully foresaw a future in which the theory would be received as the final principle of life.

However, even if the propositions:

> **he did not intend it**  
> **later generations extended it on their own**

were both true, they would not by themselves reduce the responsibility of the proposer to zero.

From Cognitive Engineering, the stronger a theory is, the greater the responsibility to make explicit:

- what question it answers,
- what it does not answer,
- which explanatory level it belongs to,
- where generalization must stop.

That responsibility becomes especially serious when the theory can become **the evaluator itself** for later explanations.

This paper therefore holds together both:

> **evaluating natural selection as an explanation of evolution**

and:

> **strongly criticizing Darwin for the fact that this explanatory system could become the most upstream premise of life-recognition and reproduce thought contamination.**

The target of criticism is not one equation or one observation result.

It is:

> **the cognitive structure in which a downstream theory is promoted into an upstream principle, and humanity mistakes an explanatory system it constructed for the world itself.**

That is the most upstream criticism.

---

# 6. The “purpose” of adaptation

Evolutionary biology sometimes describes survival or reproductive success in language resembling the “purpose” of adaptation.

This can be useful as an explanation that, because natural selection accumulates traits contributing to survival and reproduction, organisms can appear designed in that direction.

Cognitive Engineering separates:

```text
as if designed for survival/reproduction
```

from:

```text
life exists in order to survive/reproduce
```

The former is a description of evolutionary results.

The latter is a teleology concerning why life exists.

Do not mix them.

---

# 7. Scope boundary

This paper does not address:

- the concrete physicochemical mechanism of the origin of life,
- complete explanations of genes, metabolism, membranes, electric potential differences, and related mechanisms,
- mathematical reconstruction of individual models of natural selection,
- the evolutionary history of every species.

Those are targets of their respective specialist domains.

Cognitive Engineering addresses:

> **Which question is a theory answering? Is a downstream explanation being misprojected upstream?**

---

# 8. Relation to 神の領域原理（仮）

Evolutionary theory is a powerful cognition-after model.

Its success is not promoted into:

```text
the final explanation of life itself
```

Nor is evolutionary theory, as a cognition-after model, treated as if it were an evaluator that existed before cognition and finally determines what may count as a question about life, a cause, or a valid explanation.

```text
powerful explanatory system
≠
world-itself
≠
final form of the question
≠
final evaluator of other theories
```

Thought contamination is established when this boundary is lost.

The current terms “life,” “survival,” “evolution,” and “purpose” are themselves reopenable Projections.

---

# 9. Refutation and reopening conditions

This paper is re-audited at least if:

- standard evolutionary theory is shown explicitly to explain the most upstream reason for the establishment of life independently of the evolutionary process,
- the boundary sentence “life lives in order to live” is shown not to reduce confusion in the question or distinguish any explanatory level,
- another structure with greater explanatory power than the current hierarchy of life / survival / reproduction / adaptation / evolution is established,
- this paper is shown to deny the valid scope of natural selection itself,
- the five-part decomposition of “thought contamination” fails to preserve the actual cognitive constraint and a stronger decomposition is established,
- the responsibility assigned to Darwin as a theory proposer is shown to improperly mix theoretical scope with historical causality.

---

# 10. Conclusion

Natural selection, adaptation, and evolution are not denied.

However:

> **A theory that explains evolution is not the most upstream principle of the fact that life is living.**

The first problem called “Darwin's Error” in this paper is that crossing of explanatory levels.

The second, deeper problem is:

> **thought contamination, in which a downstream explanation enters perception, question formation, causality, and evaluation as an upstream premise, narrows the route by which that premise itself can be doubted, and reproduces itself through education and discourse.**

This is why the paper strongly criticizes Darwin. The criticism is not directed at the mere act of presenting natural selection as a mechanism.

> **A proposer of an explanatory system capable of constraining the cognitive frame of later generations bears responsibility for the scope, non-scope, and explanatory-level boundaries of that system.**

And to the most upstream question, “Why does life live?”, this paper answers:

> **It lives in order to live.**

This is not teleology. It is a Cognitive Engineering boundary that avoids arbitrarily adding an external purpose to life and prevents a human-constructed explanatory system from being mistaken for the world-itself.
