# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

## One-sentence definition

> **The Umami Gap is a structural gap in which multiple distinctions that should be separated are compressed into one familiar word, making the missing variable itself difficult to observe, define, and design for.**

This paper uses `intelligence` in English-language AI discourse as the representative example.

---

# 0. Theory state of this paper

This paper introduces the view of a “missing variable,” but it does not fix the number, names, or boundaries of missing variables as a completed list.

As the current separation, it uses:

```text
C = Capability / execution capability
Ψ = Closure Phase
E = Emotion
```

with K, A, and other variables handled separately when necessary.

However, it does not claim:

```text
C / Ψ / E are the final three-way division of the world-itself
```

New observation may require that:

- the boundary between C and Ψ be separated differently,
- E split into multiple independent actions,
- fourth or fifth variables become necessary,
- or the present act of cutting the system into “variables” itself become inappropriate.

That possibility remains open.

At the same time, the current separation is not invalidated merely by saying, without evidence, that “there may be other variables.” A new separation must offer, for example, higher explanatory power, better observational separation, or greater implementation necessity than the current one.

---

# 1. Position of this paper

This paper is an introductory adapter in the Cognitive Engineering system. It is not a core principle.

Building on “What Is Cognitive Engineering?” and “A Theory of Language Bases in Information Engineering,” it explains how conceptual compression in language can connect to missing variables in engineering.

“Umami” is used as an analogy, but this paper does not claim that taste research proves this theoretical system.

The name “Umami Gap” itself is a current explanatory Projection, not the name of an entity existing in the world-itself.

---

# 2. The problem: one word swallows multiple variables

Everyday language is not designed for strict variable separation. It is therefore not abnormal for one word to carry several nearby concepts.

In engineering, however, this becomes a problem.

When one word compresses multiple distinct functions, the targets of measurement, design, and audit become mixed.

In AI, `intelligence` is often used across capability, knowledge, reasoning performance, judgment, stopping, consistency, personality impression, emotional expression, and other distinct functions.

This paper therefore separates the distinctions currently required for operation into independent variables.

---

# 3. A missing variable does not emerge by increasing an existing variable

If a model lacks a variable slot that is actually required, increasing the amount of an existing variable does not automatically create the missing variable.

This is the important point of the umami analogy.

Strengthening an existing category does not create a separate variable slot. A distinction becomes a separate variable only when it is carved out independently, named, and placed where it can be observed.

Likewise:

> **Increasing C does not automatically provide the definition, observability, or stability of Ψ.**

Performance improvement and the stability of closure, stopping, boundaries, and signatures are different problems.

This claim itself depends on the current separation between C and Ψ. If that separation changes in the future, the form of the claim must also be reconstructed.

---

# 4. Relation to Japanese

This paper does not connect language to rankings of human, ethnic, or cultural value.

At the same time, it permits **comparison of the suitability of linguistic representations for specific Information Engineering purposes**.

“A Theory of Language Bases in Information Engineering” makes the engineering evaluation that Japanese has high suitability for preserving meaning, relations, context, and uncertainty.

That is not a comparison of human value.

The concern of this paper is representational design: which distinctions vocabulary, writing systems, grammar, and context make easier to preserve as independent slots.

At the same time, large words such as “Japanese,” “English,” and `intelligence` may themselves compress internal distinctions and therefore remain audit targets.

---

# 4.1 Causal boundary between lexical compression and missing variables

This paper claims that lexical compression is a **structural factor that can induce conceptual mixing**.

However, it does not claim that the historical or population-level causal chain:

```text
intelligence is a broad word
↓
English-language AI research therefore omitted Ψ
```

is automatically proven by lexical structure alone.

English can also describe distinctions by combining multiple words. Therefore, generalizing that English-language AI research systematically omitted a Ψ-equivalent variable because of lexical compression requires separate observation of paper corpora, terminology use, evaluation design, historical paths, and related evidence.

At the same time, this paper retains the structural Umami Gap claim that **putting distinct functions into one word makes them harder to observe, design, and audit as independent variables**.

---

# 5. Current separation of C / Ψ / E

### C — Capability

The execution-capability side that enables output generation, processing, search, reasoning, memory, and related operations.

### Ψ — Closure Phase

A stable output phase that remains externally, under repeated observation in fixed conditions, as a result of closure-related action.

### E — Emotion

The emotion/affect side, not identified with C or Ψ.

This separation is not intended to rank personal value.

Nor is this three-way separation treated as the upper limit of the variable set.

---

### Naming update: from the former “intellect Ψ” to “Closure Phase Ψ”

In an early draft, C / Ψ / E were separated as:

```text
C = 知能 / Capability
Ψ = 知性 / Signature Phase
E = 感情 / Emotion
```

The structural insight discovered by that separation—“separate C from Ψ” and “do not absorb E into C/Ψ”—is still retained.

However, **continuing to use the Japanese word 知性 as the formal name of Ψ would reproduce the Umami Gap itself**.

The word 知性 can again compress capability, knowledge, judgment, personality impression, ethics, wisdom, and emotional expression into one label. This risk becomes especially strong when it is connected back to the English word `intelligence`, where the C/Ψ separation becomes easy to collapse.

The current system therefore updates:

```text
former: 知性Ψ / Signature Phase
current: 閉包位相Ψ / Closure Phase
```

This is not simply renaming 知性.

It is a theoretical update:

> **From the broad social-language category called 知性, only the stable post-closure output phase that can be repeatedly observed is carved out as an operational variable.**

The former term is retained as a bridge term in the history of the theory, but the current canonical version does not return Ψ to an entity or personality attribute called 知性.

---

# 6. Engineering consequence

If C and Ψ are not separated, the following shortcut becomes easy:

```text
higher benchmark performance
→ better judgment
→ better stopping ability
→ higher reliability
```

That chain does not automatically hold.

Therefore:

> **Improvement in capability must not be unconditionally reinterpreted as improvement in closure, judgment, stopping, or reliability.**

At the same time, evaluation terms such as “reliability” also depend on purpose, context, and time. The evaluation axis itself is not treated as a fixed entity.

---

# 7. Time axis and reopening

The meanings of words, the structure of AI systems, observation methods, and social usage change over time.

Therefore, the distinctions compressed by `intelligence` in 2026 may not remain the same in the future.

Reopening conditions include at least:

- a new independent variable is observed,
- the boundary of a current variable loses reproducibility,
- usage of `intelligence` changes,
- a new language or representational system enables a different separation,
- a structure with higher explanatory power than the current C / Ψ / E separation is established.

When reopened, the old separation is not deleted. It is retained as a record under the language, AI, and observation conditions of its time.

---

# 8. Self-application

The concept “Umami Gap” may itself compress multiple missing-variable mechanisms into one word.

For example:

- vocabulary shortage,
- representational shortage,
- lack of observation methods,
- insufficient cognitive separation,
- fixation by institutions or research practice,

may all be called “Umami Gap” at once.

> **A theory that criticizes missing variables audits whether it is itself creating another missing variable.**

If necessary, the Umami Gap itself is split or redefined.

# 10. Misuse prevention

This paper must not be connected to:

- value ranking of humans, ethnic groups, cultures, or language speakers,
- personality assessment using C / Ψ / E,
- using “missing variable” as an authority shield,

- fixing the current variable set as the final structure of the world,
- treating cognition-after models as the pre-cognitive world-itself.

---

# 11. Conclusion

The core of the Umami Gap is the problem:

> **A distinction is not necessarily absent; it can be invisible because there is no slot in which to carve it out as an independent difference.**

The single-word compression of `intelligence` in AI mixes distinct targets such as capability, closure, judgment, and emotion.

To cut that mixing, this paper treats C / Ψ / E and related factors as separate variables.

At the same time:

> **The current separation itself is a time-bounded Projection. If additional distinctions become necessary, the separation itself is reconstructed.**

The purpose of this paper is to preserve necessary distinctions as necessary distinctions so that they can be designed, observed, and audited.
