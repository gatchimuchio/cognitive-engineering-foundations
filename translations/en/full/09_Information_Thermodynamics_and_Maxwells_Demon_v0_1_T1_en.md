# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

# 1. Problem setting

Information thermodynamics places Maxwell's demon, measurement, feedback, memory, erasure, mutual information, work, heat, free energy, and related concepts within one theoretical vocabulary.

Standard literature treats Maxwell's demon as a thought experiment and describes relations between information processing and thermodynamic quantities through Landauer's principle, mutual information, nonequilibrium statistical mechanics, and related frameworks.

This paper does not ask whether those equations are usable.

It separates the following question:

> **Did what we call “information” exist independently on the side of the physical system, or is it a descriptive label assigned by an observer to state differences, correlations, and usability?**

---

# 2. Decomposition from Cognitive Engineering

If the physical apparatus is completely closed, at least the following exist:

- physical system,
- particles / states,
- interactions,
- sensor,
- memory medium,
- controller,
- actuator,
- power source,
- reset process,
- heat,
- work,
- energy transfer,
- correlations,
- time evolution.

These can be observed as physical states and physical processes.

By contrast, statements such as:

- this is one bit,
- information was obtained,
- information was erased,
- information was converted into work,

become established after an observer classifies differences, correlations, and functions of physical states through a specified descriptive system.

Therefore the establishment order:

```text
physical state
→ observation of difference
→ system partition
→ state classification
→ description of correlation
→ functional use
→ label “information”
```

must be audited.

---

# 3. The “fiction” of Maxwell's demon

Calling it a “fictional demon” here does not mean that Maxwell's demon is fraudulent or worthless.

It means:

> **The demon is a construct in a thought experiment that compresses system boundaries, measurement, recording, control, and selection into one anthropomorphized subject.**

If the demon is placed as:

```text
observer
recorder
judge
door operator
```

then the description “the demon obtained information” appears natural.

But if the demon apparatus is closed to include:

```text
sensor
+ physical memory
+ control
+ actuator
+ power source
+ reset
+ controlled system
```

what remains is physical states, correlations, control, interactions, and energy transfer.

Therefore:

> **Before treating the demon as introducing a new natural phenomenon, audit whether ordinary physical processes have merely been redescribed in information vocabulary.**

---

# 4. Position of Landauer's principle

Landauer's principle concerns thermodynamic constraints when logically irreversible operations are implemented in physical media.

This paper does not deny that physical constraint.

However:

```text
physical implementation of information erasure has thermodynamic cost
```

does not automatically imply:

```text
information itself is an independent physical entity
```

The physical cost of changing a medium's state is separated from the cognitive label “information” assigned to the state difference.

> **Landauer's principle constrains physical operation. It is not, by itself, an ontological bridge in which an entity called information is converted into energy.**

---

# 5. Decomposing “information to work”

Information thermodynamics describes relations between mutual information and extractable work, among other relations.

From Cognitive Engineering, this is decomposed as:

```text
physical states are correlated
↓
an observer can distinguish and record the correlation
↓
control operation uses the correlation
↓
work is extracted under conditions where it would otherwise be unavailable
↓
the useful correlation is called “information”
```

Thus:

```text
information → work
```

is not read as an ontology in which an independent informational substance is converted into work.

This paper separates:

> **work was extracted from a physical system in which correlations could be used**

from the descriptive operation:

> **that correlation was called information.**

---

# 6. Establishment judgment on information thermodynamics

If information is:

> **a descriptive quantity in which an observer carves out physical states, differences, correlations, and relations according to purpose, classification, and usability,**

then the effective portions of information thermodynamics can be redescribed through combinations of:

- statistical mechanics,
- nonequilibrium thermodynamics,
- stochastic processes,
- control,
- measurement,
- correlation,
- physical memory.

In that case:

> **“information thermodynamics” may function as a useful cross-disciplinary label, but it does not hold as an independent natural science whose target is an independent natural object called information.**

This is the Cognitive Engineering establishment judgment of this paper.

---

# 7. What this paper does not deny

This paper does not deny:

- the second law,
- statistical mechanics,
- nonequilibrium processes,
- physical tests of Landauer's principle,
- useful mathematical descriptions using mutual information,
- feedback control,
- experimentally observed work, heat, or probability distributions.

What it rejects is:

> **the cognitive operation that promotes “information” into a natural object existing independently of cognition because those results succeeded.**

---

# 8. Relation to 神の領域原理（仮）

```text
physical state
≠
information description constructed about the physical state
```

A cognition-after world-image containing “information,” “bit,” “correlation resource,” or “information to work” is not promoted into a constituent that exists as-is in the pre-cognitive world-itself.

---

# 9. Refutation and reopening conditions

This paper is re-audited at least if:

- “information” is identified as a unique independent physical quantity that does not depend on observer system partition, state classification, or meaning assignment,
- a physical effect is demonstrated that cannot be explained without introducing an information ontology and cannot be isomorphically reconstructed through ordinary physical states, correlations, and control,
- the system-boundary closure used in this paper omits an important observable in existing experiments,
- the separation between “information” and “physical correlation” itself fails observationally.

---

# 10. Conclusion

The core of this paper is simple:

> **Being able to describe physics using information is different from information being an independent physical entity.**

If Maxwell's demon is closed as a complete physical apparatus, ordinary physical processes remain: sensing, memory, control, correlation, energy transfer, reset, and related operations.

Cognitive Engineering therefore first closes the physical process and then audits what the label “information” is compressing.
