# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

# 1. The success of relativity and the nature of time and space are separate questions

Relativity describes time, distance, simultaneity across observers, spacetime geometry in gravitational fields, and related phenomena with high precision.

This paper does not dispute that success.

It questions the promotion:

```text
observed values agree with the theory
↓
therefore the underlying nature of time and space is exactly as described by the theory
```

> **The consistency of an observational model is not identical to the target carved out by that model being the pre-cognitive world-itself.**

---

# 2. Audit time and space separately

Cognitive Engineering does not place time and space as the same kind of thing from the beginning.

Under the current separation:

### Time

is treated as coordinates/relations describing:

- change,
- order,
- before/after,
- duration,
- history,
- updating,
- ordering of state transitions.

### Space

is treated as coordinates/relations describing:

- place,
- arrangement,
- distance,
- direction,
- locality,
- positional relations among targets.

Therefore the starting point is:

```text
time
≠
space
```

The fact that relativity can unify temporal and spatial coordinates into a single spacetime model does not automatically prove:

```text
time and space were the same ontological kind before cognition
```

---

# 3. Spacetime is a powerful Projection

Relativity treats events as points in spacetime, and the division into space and time can vary with the observer.

This is an extremely powerful description.

From Cognitive Engineering, spacetime is positioned as:

> **a high-performance cognition-after Projection for handling events, distances, ordering, and causal relations consistently across different observers.**

This evaluation does not weaken relativity.

Rather, it separates:

```text
strong descriptive model
≠
final ontology of the world-itself
```

---

# 4. What is “relative”?

In relativity, assignment of:

- simultaneity,
- time coordinates,
- spatial coordinates,
- measured distance,

can vary with the state of motion or related conditions of the observer.

Cognitive Engineering separates:

> **what was observed,**  
> **what was assigned as coordinates,**  
> **which relations were retained as invariants.**

The everyday-language expression “time is relative” is not shortcut into an entity image:

> **a substance called time stretches and contracts.**

The physical statement that clocks following different paths or gravitational conditions show different proper times is separated from an ontology of “time itself.”

---

# 5. Coordinates are not the world-itself

Coordinates are a method of assigning values to observed events so that relations can be compared.

Therefore:

```text
time coordinates
spatial coordinates
inertial frames
coordinate transformations
```

are descriptive structures adopted by humans to handle observational results consistently.

Their high-precision agreement with observation matters.

But:

> **The success of a coordinate system is not converted into proof that the coordinates themselves are engraved into the world-itself.**

---

# 6. Separate mathematical unification from ontological identity

The ability of one mathematical structure to handle multiple observables in a unified way is a major theoretical success.

However:

```text
can be handled in the same equations
≠
are the same ontological kind
```

The mathematical unification of time and space as spacetime does not imply:

```text
time = space
```

as an ontology.

The question of this paper is:

> **Has unification in description been promoted into identity of the targets themselves?**

---

# 7. Cognitive Engineering questions to relativity

The questions this paper asks are:

- What was carved out as an event?
- Why are temporal and spatial coordinates placed in this form?
- What is an observer?
- What was held invariant?
- How far does agreement among observations guarantee target identity?
- Has the possibility of an isomorphic redescription of the same observations outside the spacetime model been excluded?
- Has coordinate description been promoted into the world-itself?

These questions are not asked in order to declare “relativity is wrong.”

> **They audit the boundary so that the questions relativity successfully answered are not expanded into an ontology it did not answer.**

---

# 8. Relation to 神の領域原理（仮）

神の領域原理（仮） holds the boundary:

> **Do not assert a world-image established after cognition as the pre-cognitive world-itself.**

The spacetime model of relativity is not exempt.

Relativity may be adopted extremely strongly for local use.

But:

```text
relativity explains observations
≠
the ontology of time, space, and spacetime has been finally settled
```

---

# 9. Bridge to the quantum-gravity problem

This separation matters in quantum gravity.

General relativity and quantum theory need not assign the same roles to:

- time,
- space,
- state,
- observation,
- background,
- causality.

The fact that both theories use the words “space” and “time” is therefore not enough to fix them as descriptions of the same target.

That issue is treated separately in document 12.

---

# 10. Refutation and reopening conditions

This paper is re-audited at least if:

- separating time and space as different cognitive types is shown observationally or theoretically to be unnecessary,
- the spacetime model is directly identified, beyond descriptive convenience, as the unique structure of the world-itself independent of cognitive operation,
- the separation in this paper is shown to misdescribe observational facts of relativity,
- another structure with greater explanatory power than the current distinction among time, space, and spacetime is established.

---

# 11. Conclusion

This paper does not deny relativity.

What it rejects is:

> **promoting success in observation and mathematics directly into the final ontology of time, space, and spacetime.**

Cognitive Engineering first separates:

```text
time = relation describing change, order, and history
space = relation describing place, arrangement, and distance
spacetime = powerful cognition-after model that handles both in a unified way
```

It then audits what is observational fact, what is coordinate, what is model, and what is inference about the world-itself.
