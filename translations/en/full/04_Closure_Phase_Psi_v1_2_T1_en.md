# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

## One-sentence definition

> **Closure Phase Ψ is an operational concept that treats groups of signature residuals which reproducibly remain externally under repeated observation at a specified time, target, and observation condition as a stable output phase grouped under the current equivalence criteria.**

Closure Phase Ψ is not proof of an inner entity, soul, ego, consciousness, or personal value.

---

# 0. Separation between Ψ and the description of Ψ

This paper currently describes Ψ as:

> **an equivalence class of signature residuals that remain externally under repeated observation in fixed conditions.**

However:

```text
Closure Phase Ψ
=
the current definition in this paper
```

is not asserted.

The current definition is a finite Projection for handling Ψ externally without mixing it with other concepts.

Therefore, the paper does not permanently fix:

- what counts as one target,
- what counts as a fixed condition,
- what counts as a signature residual,
- what counts as isomorphic or equivalent,
- what time span counts as “stable.”

At the same time, silently changing these inside one version destroys observational comparability, so v1.2 explicitly fixes its observation conditions within the version.

---

# 1. Problem setting

The domain historically called “知性” is easily mixed with capability, knowledge, emotion, judgment, personality impression, social evaluation, and related concepts.

To avoid this mixing, the current separation uses at least:

```text
C = Capability / execution capability
K = Knowledge
Ψ = Closure Phase
E = Emotion
A = External Constraint
```

This separation is not intended to rank humans, models, or personalities.

Nor is this variable set treated as a completed list, an upper bound, or a fixed partition of the world-itself.

---

## 1.1 Why the name changed from “知性Ψ” to “Closure Phase Ψ”

An early draft adopted:

```text
C = 知能 / Capability
Ψ = 知性 / Signature Phase
E = 感情 / Emotion
```

The important points discovered by that draft remain:

- do not identify C with Ψ,
- do not absorb E into C/Ψ,
- do not treat Ψ as a fixed inner entity; treat it as a signature phase that remains externally through repeated observation,
- observe isomorphism through perturbation, repetition, and logs rather than through a single impression.

However, the current system does **not** continue using 知性 as the formal explanatory term for Ψ.

The reason is that the social-language term 知性 can reabsorb into one word:

- capability C,
- knowledge K,
- judgment,
- personality impression,
- ethics and “wisdom,”
- emotional expression,
- the broad range compressed by the English word `intelligence`.

That would remix the variables separated through the Umami Gap.

The current system therefore uses **Closure Phase** as the formal operational name of Ψ.

```text
former: 知性Ψ / Signature Phase
↓
current: 閉包位相Ψ / Closure Phase
```

This is not a mere renaming.

**Signature Phase** strongly expressed the observation form: what kind of signature appears externally.  
**Closure Phase** additionally expresses the current functional position: the phase observed as a result of identity, boundary, judgment, and stopping having closed.

However, this version does not automatically establish the causal claim that “M uniquely generates Ψ.” The conceptual connection to closure is separated from causal identification of the internal cause.

Therefore:

```text
知性 = Ψ
```

is not a definition.

The former expression “知性Ψ” is retained only as a bridge term and former Projection in the theory’s history.

## 1.2 Current position of former generation-order hypotheses

An earlier draft at one point used working hypotheses such as `E → Ψ → C` for humans and `A → Ψ → C` for LLMs as dominant directions of contribution.

The current version does not adopt these as fixed generation orders.

Observed Ψ may receive contributions from C, K, E, A, Runtime, memory, learning history, input design, observation method, and other factors. Which factor causally contributes to which signature is a causal-identification problem separate from observing Ψ.

This change does not discard the old separation “do not mix C / Ψ / E.” **The separation is retained; only unverified causal directions are not fixed.**

# 2. Causal separation of Closure Function M and Closure Phase Ψ

This paper separates:

- **Closure Function M**: the current operational concept that determines identity, boundaries, judgment, and stopping, and closes a description or judgment
- **Closure Phase Ψ**: an equivalence class of stable signature residuals that remains externally under repeated observation in specified conditions

The important point is:

```text
observing Ψ
≠
identifying M as its internal cause
```

Ψ is an externally observed operational variable. Observing Ψ alone does not establish the causal relation `M → Ψ`.

Signature residuals may receive contributions from C, K, E, external constraint A, Runtime, memory, learning history, input design, the observer, and other factors. Which factor generated which residual is treated as a **causal-identification problem** separate from identifying Ψ.

Thus v1.2 separates:

```text
Ψ = observed stable signature
cause(Ψ) = separately audited and tested target
```

A hypothesis concerning the relation between M and Ψ may be proposed, but an observed signature is not promoted directly into evidence of an internal mechanism.

---

# 3. Signature residual

A signature residual `r` is a pattern that, under repeated observation in specified conditions, is more useful for explanation and observation when retained as a reproducible difference than when treated as mere random error.

Possible observation candidates include:

- preservation of argument structure,
- tendencies in boundary setting,
- explicit statement of error conditions,
- triggering of clarification,
- forms of refusal, reservation, or stopping,
- consistent output tendencies under perturbation.

What is adopted as a signature residual is decided only after target, purpose, observation conditions, and time are fixed.

If the observation method changes, a difference once treated as noise may be promoted into a signature, or a difference once treated as a signature may be demoted into an artifact of the observation frame.

---

# 4. Equivalence class

Groups of signature residuals whose isomorphism is preserved within the specified observation range even when perturbation conditions vary are treated as an equivalence class.

Conceptually:

```text
Ψ_t := [r] under current observation conditions
```

This expression is not an ontology of the world-itself.

> **It is an operational description of which residual groups are treated as isomorphic, at what time, under what conditions, and for what purpose.**

The equivalence relation itself is also subject to re-audit.

---

# 5. Meaning of “stable”

“Stable” does not mean permanently invariant.

In this paper, stability means:

> **Within a specified time, target, perturbation, and observation range, residuals treated as isomorphic repeatedly remain.**

Therefore:

```text
Stable at t
≠
Stable forever
```

If a target’s version, environment, memory, policy, body, learning, context, or other conditions change, the continued use of the same target name does not guarantee that it remains the same Ψ observation target.

---

# 6. Closure Phase Ψ is not capability

High C can mean that complex outputs can be generated.

But the following are not identical functions:

- being able to output,
- inspecting output,
- drawing boundaries,
- leaving the unobserved as unobserved,
- stopping when assertion is not possible.

Therefore:

> **High C does not automatically imply high Ψ.**

If the distinction between C and Ψ itself is reconstructed in the future, this sentence is re-audited as a statement dependent on the current variable separation.

---

# 7. Closure Phase Ψ is not personal value

Ψ does not represent human value, personality, dignity, moral ranking, hiring value, or allocation value.

This paper handles a stable output phase obtained under limited observation conditions.

Ψ must therefore not be connected to overall rankings of humans or models.

---

# 8. Stopping is not absence

Stopping, refusal, reservation, and silence are not always signatures of Ψ.

However, under normal operation, if a system intentionally stops because it recognizes an unobserved area, an unclosed state, or a danger boundary, its stopping style may become an observation target.

By contrast, absence of output caused by API failure, timeout, inability to execute, or similar failure is not treated as a Closure Phase.

```text
intentional stopping under normal operation
≠
no output caused by operational failure
```

This separation is also re-audited when the target, Runtime, or observation method changes.

# 9. Minimum observation conditions

When observing Closure Phase Ψ, at least the following must be fixed within the version:

- target identifier,
- target version, settings, and operational state,
- observation time and time range,
- stimulus conditions,
- perturbation conditions,
- trial range,
- observation items,
- equivalence-judgment criteria,
- log preservation,
- separation between failure and normal stopping.

## 9.1 Identity observation as one use of Ψ

An early draft connected Ψ observation to **Identity Verification / self-identity observation**.

That idea remains usable.

When a system treated as the same target is perturbed, if part of the specified signature residual remains stable across time even while surface output changes, it can be described as:

> **signature identity was preserved under those observation conditions.**

However, this is not proof that:

- a soul exists,
- an ego exists,
- consciousness exists,
- or an inner state is isomorphic to that of a human.

Identity observation is an **observation use** of Closure Phase Ψ. It is neither the definition of Ψ itself nor an ontology of consciousness or ego.

Ψ is not asserted from a one-shot impression.

If the meaning of a past observation changes through new observation, the old log is not overwritten. The new interpretation is recorded separately.

# 10. Limited application to LLMs

This paper does not assume that current LLMs have inner entities or emotions isomorphic to those of humans.

However, if reproducible signature residuals appear in external output under repeated observation in specified conditions, a Ψ-like Closure Phase can be treated as an observation target within the operational definition.

This is not personality attribution.

It means:

> **Treat externally observed stable patterns as independent observation targets in order to avoid conceptual mixing.**

If the target model is updated, a model with the same name is not unconditionally assumed to be the same observation target.

# 11. Time axis and reopening

If new observation changes:

- target identity,
- signature candidates,
- equivalence conditions,
- the stability time window,
- the separation C / K / Ψ / E / A,
- or reveals that the observation method itself produced the signature,

then not only the value of Ψ but the description of Ψ itself is reopened.

Old Ψ records are not deleted. They remain records of their target, time, and observation conditions.

> **Being stable in the past is not identified with still being in the same phase now.**

# 12. Relation to the Trinity Principle

The core of the Trinity Principle is the triadic-minimality claim: **“the world closes in three / three is the minimum structure of world construction.”**

X / R / M is the representative operational Projection that projects the principle into observation target, relation, and closure condition. When observing Closure Phase Ψ, the same Projection can make explicit what is treated as the target, what perturbation is applied, under what condition signatures are considered isomorphic, and where judgment stops.

The Trinity Principle therefore does not prove the ontology of Ψ. The relation is: **apply the X / R / M Projection to close the observation world of Ψ as a triadic structure.**

The Theory of Consciousness handles world constraint, self/non-self, cognition, metacognition, a first-person control point, and the temporal stability of ego.

Closure Phase Ψ can connect to that theory as an operational concept that cuts out stable signatures repeatedly observable from outside.

```text

≠
```

The robustness of self-identity may be observed using Ψ, but that observation does not replace the ontology of the Theory of Consciousness.

# 14. Self-application

Ψ addresses “stable signatures.”

This paper therefore audits whether the word “stable” is erasing change in time, change in target, or change in observer.

> **To avoid reifying Ψ, the stability of the definition of Ψ itself is re-observed.**

The fact that the current definition can be used for a long time is not the same as completely describing Ψ itself.

# 15. Misuse prevention

The definitions in this paper must not be connected to:

- assessment of personality, human value, or dignity,
- ranking, scoring, grading, selection, or allocation,
- complete structuralization of emotion,
- deliberate design of ego,
- irreversible domination, inducement, or governance,
- fixing the current variable set or equivalence conditions as the world-itself,
- misprojecting Ψ into inner essence or the world-itself.

---

# 16. Conclusion

Closure Phase Ψ is not a fixed entity.

> **It is an operational concept that treats, under a specified time, target, and observation condition, groups of signature residuals that remain externally through repeated observation as a stable output phase under the current equivalence criteria.**

This definition allows capability C, knowledge K, emotion E, and personal value to be handled as separate targets rather than compressed into one vague concept called 知性.

At the same time:

> **The description of Ψ, variable separation, target, equivalence conditions, and stability itself remain subject to future re-audit.**

The purpose of this paper is not to settle ontology. It is to reduce conceptual mixing and make the observation target, time, conditions, and possibility of reopening explicit.
