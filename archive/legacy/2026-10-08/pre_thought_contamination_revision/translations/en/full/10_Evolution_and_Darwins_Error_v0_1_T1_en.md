> **Position**: This paper does not deny natural selection, adaptation, inheritance, or evolution as phenomena. From Cognitive Engineering, it audits whether “a theory that explains evolution” is being identified with “the most upstream explanation of the fact that life is living.”

> **Terminology rule**: “Darwin's Error” in this paper is not a historical claim assigning blanket responsibility to everything Darwin himself wrote. It is a Cognitive Engineering name for **the crossing of explanatory levels in which the downstream evolutionary mechanism of natural selection is promoted into the most upstream principle of life itself**.

# Part I. Full English Translation of the Japanese Canonical Original

# 1. What is accepted and what is questioned

Evolutionary theory addresses variation across generations, inheritance, selection, reproductive success, population change, and related processes.

Natural selection is a powerful framework for explaining how heritable variation that creates differences in survival and reproduction can be retained or changed within populations.

This paper does not deny that.

The problem is the leap:

```text
can explain evolution
↓
therefore has explained why life lives
```

---

# 2. Separate upstream and downstream

Under the current cognition of this paper, at least the following are separated:

```text
life is living
↓
an individual maintains itself
↓
survival / action / environmental response
↓
reproduction
↓
generational difference
↓
natural selection
↓
adaptation
↓
evolution
```

This is not claimed to be the one and only biological process chart.

What matters is the explanatory hierarchy:

> **Do not place evolution, adaptation, or reproduction upstream of the state/process that “life is living.”**

Evolution explains change occurring in populations of living systems.

It presupposes a world in which life is already alive, individuals are maintained, and generations continue.

---

# 3. “Life lives in order to live”

The most upstream expression in this paper is:

> **Life lives in order to live.**

This is not teleology introducing an external purpose.

It does not claim:

```text
life has a purpose assigned by the universe
```

It claims the opposite.

> **As long as it is life, it continues the state/process called living. A question that requires an external purpose beyond that is itself adding an explanatory level outside life without justification.**

Thus “life lives in order to live” is neither a predictive model nor an evolutionary mechanism.

It is:

> **a boundary sentence for auditing whether the question “why does life live?” is silently demanding a purpose outside life itself.**

---

# 4. Do not identify living with “what survives remains”

“Living” and “what survived remains” are not the same sentence.

```text
life is living
```

refers to an ongoing state/process.

By contrast:

```text
variants that survive and reproduce remain in the population
```

refers to the result of selection.

If the latter is used to explain the former, the explanation easily slides into self-reference:

> **Life lives because what survived survived.**

Natural selection can explain what remains within a population.

That explanation must not be promoted directly into the answer to “why does life live?”

---

# 5. “Darwin's Error”

What this paper calls “Darwin's Error” is:

> **promoting downstream results or mechanisms—adaptation, reproduction, natural selection—into the most upstream cause, purpose, or reason for the existence of life.**

This is not a denial of the theory of natural selection itself.

Separate:

```text
natural selection explains evolution
```

from:

```text
natural selection explains why life lives
```

The former is a biological question.

The latter crosses explanatory levels.

---

# 6. The “purpose” of adaptation

Evolutionary biology sometimes describes survival or reproductive success in language resembling the “purpose” of adaptation.

This can be useful as an explanation that, because natural selection accumulates traits contributing to survival and reproduction, organisms can appear designed in that direction.

Cognitive Engineering separates:

```text
as if designed for survival/reproduction
```

from:

```text
life exists in order to survive/reproduce
```

The former is a description of evolutionary results.

The latter is a teleology concerning why life exists.

Do not mix them.

---

# 7. Scope boundary

This paper does not address:

- the concrete physicochemical mechanism of the origin of life,
- complete explanations of genes, metabolism, membranes, electric potential differences, and related mechanisms,
- mathematical reconstruction of individual models of natural selection,
- the evolutionary history of every species.

Those are targets of their respective specialist domains.

Cognitive Engineering addresses:

> **Which question is a theory answering? Is a downstream explanation being misprojected upstream?**

---

# 8. Relation to 神の領域原理（仮）

Evolutionary theory is a powerful cognition-after model.

Its success is not promoted into:

```text
the final explanation of life itself
```

The current terms “life,” “survival,” “evolution,” and “purpose” are themselves reopenable Projections.

---

# 9. Refutation and reopening conditions

This paper is re-audited at least if:

- standard evolutionary theory is shown explicitly to explain the most upstream reason for the establishment of life independently of the evolutionary process,
- the boundary sentence “life lives in order to live” is shown not to reduce confusion in the question or distinguish any explanatory level,
- another structure with greater explanatory power than the current hierarchy of life / survival / reproduction / adaptation / evolution is established,
- this paper is shown to deny the valid scope of natural selection itself.

---

# 10. Conclusion

Natural selection, adaptation, and evolution are not denied.

However:

> **A theory that explains evolution is not the most upstream principle of the fact that life is living.**

What this paper calls “Darwin's Error” is crossing that explanatory boundary.

And to the most upstream question, “Why does life live?”, this paper answers:

> **It lives in order to live.**

This is not teleology. It is a Cognitive Engineering boundary against arbitrarily adding an external purpose to life.
